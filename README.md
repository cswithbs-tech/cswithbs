## 📌 What is CSwithBS?

**CSwithBS** is a personal academic platform built to make learning Computer Science structured, clear, and genuinely useful — for university students, GATE aspirants, and engineers who want real depth.

Most CS resources are either too shallow or scattered across dozens of sites. This platform organizes knowledge **Subject → Chapter → Topic**, so learners always know exactly where they are in the curriculum and what comes next — similar to how GFG or Khan Academy structure their content, but built from the ground up with a premium reading experience.

---

## 🎯 Goals

- Organize core CS subjects (DBMS, OS, DSA, CN, and more) into structured, chapter-by-chapter notes
- Publish in-depth technical blog articles and opinion pieces
- Provide a newsletter and community around computer science learning
- Offer a seamless, rich authoring experience for the writer through a private Writers Hub

---

## ✨ Features

### 🎓 Courses & Notes

- Subjects organized by chapters, each containing ordered topic notes
- Subject cards with chapter previews (GFG-inspired)
- Full note reader with Prev/Next topic navigation
- Collapsible sidebar showing full course syllabus

### 📝 Technical Blog

- Long-form articles with rich content (code blocks, tables, math, embeds)
- Category filtering, tags, search, views, likes, and bookmarks
- Comment system and reader interaction

### 🖊️ Writers Hub

- Universal TipTap-powered rich text editor for blog posts and notes
- Subject and Chapter management for the curriculum
- Draft auto-save, revision history, scheduled publishing

### 👤 User System

- Authentication via NextAuth
- Role hierarchy: `USER → PREMIUM → WRITER → ADMIN → SUPER_ADMIN`
- Profile pages, bookmarks, and notification settings
- Premium membership tier

### 🔧 Admin Panel

- Analytics dashboard (views, likes, top posts)
- User management with role and premium controls
- Newsletter system with subscriber management and email delivery
- Comment moderation and media management
